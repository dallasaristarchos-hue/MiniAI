Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how MiniAI handles information when you use the MiniAI website and Android app at miniai-ai.pages.dev.

Information stored on your device

Chat history, the active conversation, and preferences are saved in browser or app local storage on your device. MiniAI does not currently sync chat history to a MiniAI account. You can delete conversations in the app or clear app storage. Uninstalling the Android app may remove its local app data.

Information sent to MiniAI and service providers

When you send a message, recent conversation context and attachments you choose are sent over HTTPS to MiniAI's Cloudflare Worker to generate a response using Cloudflare Workers AI. For current information, the service may retrieve public webpages and search results from providers such as Bing or Google News. If you provide a webpage URL, the service may fetch that public page. Images you attach for analysis or editing are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or information you do not want processed by these providers.

Voice and microphone

Voice input uses speech-recognition features provided by the browser or Android environment. The Android app requests microphone access only when a voice feature asks for it. Speech recognition may be processed by the browser or operating-system speech provider under that provider's terms. MiniAI does not intentionally record or store microphone audio on its own servers. Text chat works without microphone access.

MiniAI accounts and ratings

Optional accounts require an account name and password. MiniAI stores the account name, display name, a salted password hash, and hashed session tokens in Cloudflare D1. No email address is requested. If you submit a rating, the rating and optional feedback are stored with your account to enforce one rating per account. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

Reports

If you report an AI response, the report category and response excerpt are stored in Cloudflare D1 so the developer can review reported content. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in report explanations.

Storage, security, and retention

MiniAI uses Cloudflare infrastructure for its Worker API and D1 database. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Account and rating data remain until you delete your account or the service is retired. Deleting your account removes its account record, active sessions, and associated rating and feedback. Reports are retained for review and safety improvement and may be deleted periodically. Chat history remains on your device until you delete it there; deleting an account does not delete local chat history. Providers that process requests may handle data under their own privacy terms and retention practices.

Sharing

MiniAI does not sell personal information or use third-party advertising SDKs. Information is shared with infrastructure and AI/search providers only as needed to operate features you request, or when required by law.

Children and safety

MiniAI is a general-purpose AI service and is not designed as a child-directed app. AI responses can be inaccurate or inappropriate. Do not rely on MiniAI for emergencies or professional medical, legal, or financial advice. Use the in-app Report control to flag an offensive response.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear local conversations and settings in the app. You can delete an optional MiniAI account inside Settings or through the external account deletion page. Account deletion removes the account, sessions, and associated rating data. It does not erase local chat history or copies already processed by third-party providers.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, use the project's GitHub issue page and do not post passwords, personal information, or private chat content. This policy may be updated when MiniAI's features or providers change; the effective date above will be revised.
